# SpecForge 10-minute demo plan

**Status:** Script prepared; no recording has been made. This script reflects the currently verified application state as of 2026-11-20. It does not present blocked AI workflows as successful.

## Opening

“Software teams often receive requirements that sound clear but leave important behavior undecided. SpecForge helps teams validate requirements deterministically, surface semantic questions with AI, and keep every proposed change reviewable and tied to a requirement version. Deterministic rules validate. AI analyzes and recommends. People review and approve.”

## Preparation and recording checklist

- Start Docker Desktop, the Compose stack, and Ollama. Confirm `/api/health/ready`, `/api/ai/health`, and the configured Qwen model before recording.
- Use a clean browser window at a consistent desktop viewport. Confirm the app does not show credentials or personal data.
- Keep a successful, persisted analysis and refinement available; these are genuine local-model outputs. If inference fails during a later recording, show the saved results and label them as a previously verified run.
- Do not record a successful acceptance or test-case generation: neither has been verified for this demo state.
- Never invent criteria, tests, or coverage. If the failed generation screen is shown, explain the guard rejection plainly.
- Record a 10-minute cap only after measuring the resulting video. No video exists yet.
